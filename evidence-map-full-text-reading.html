The analysis of the 201 included studies

This page holds the analysis of the 201 studies included in the FAR-SIDS systematic review. Seven tabs set out what repeats across the literature, how aid and remittances reach people, what the 2027 household survey has to measure, which methods this data can support, and where the studies disagree with each other. Every point names the paper it came from and the file it was extracted into. The review closed on 12 August 2026, and I revised the analysis on 20 August 2026 against the four blind corpus readings and the adversarial review, adding thirteen themes, four survey variables, two methods and two contradictions. Where several papers report the same underlying study, their findings are counted once. Four papers drawn from one doctorate and published in four journals, each framed a little differently but resting on the same data, are one piece of evidence rather than four. Figures on this page are recorded as the readers found them, and every one is checked against the paper it came from before it appears in any public document.
