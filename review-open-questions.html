Thought provoking questions

I read 270 full texts for the FAR-SIDS systematic review between July and August 2026. Some of them answered questions I had. Others raised questions none of the 270 answers, and those are the ones collected here.

Each question came out of a particular study, or out of two studies that disagree with each other. I have written down why it came up and which papers are behind it, so anyone can follow it back and decide for themselves. Some of these will become hypotheses the 2027 fieldwork can test, some will turn out to have been answered in work the review did not reach, and some will stay open.

Click a question to read it. I add new ones as I keep reading.
