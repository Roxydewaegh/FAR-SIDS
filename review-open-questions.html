Thought provoking questions

Each question came out of a particular study, or out of two studies that disagree with each other. I have written down why it came up and which papers are behind it, so anyone can follow it back and decide for themselves. Some of these will become hypotheses the 2027 fieldwork can test.

Click a question to read it. I add new ones as I keep reading.
