Sources, named on every row: the OECD Creditor Reporting System, the World Bank projects database, the Lowy Institute Pacific Aid Map, IATI, national ministry registers, budget statements and named press reports. A programme whose only record is a single commitment year in a donor register is marked to confirm, because a commitment records money rather than operation, and only a key informant can say whether it runs. Programme types follow the World Bank ASPIRE classification, extended where this project needs categories it does not separate. Programmes grouped under one name are read out once, under that name.
