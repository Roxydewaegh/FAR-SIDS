FAR-SIDS global shock ledger

The global shock factor G, in its two built forms: world output growth and the dated crisis years. G is what every country is exposed to at the same time, and its job in every model is to be removed, so that whatever remains between the aid and remittance series is not their shared response to the world. It is the input the common shock pre-test runs on, and that pre-test decides whether the macro strand estimates a relationship or becomes descriptive.

What the two flows did in those episodes, in the project's own data

  • Remittances
  • Net aid

The change in each flow over each episode, in current US dollars, from the project's own series: remittances are the series each country's models estimate on, and aid is net official development assistance received. In 2008 to 2009 remittances fell and aid rose in all three countries. In 2019 to 2021 remittances rose strongly everywhere and aid more than doubled in Comoros; Tongan aid rose 51 per cent in 2020 and gave most of it back in 2021, and Jamaican net aid fell across the episode. Read alone, the first episode looks like substitution and the second looks like complementarity, and that reversal is what the pre-test removes before any estimate is believed.

A sample containing both episodes averages them and reports the average as though it were a relationship between the two flows, when it is a relationship between each of them and the world. That is the error the pre-test exists to catch, and it is why the decision rule is fixed before any estimate is seen: if the aid coefficient survives conditioning on G, the strand estimates as designed; if it collapses toward zero, what the literature calls substitution is shock covariance and the strand becomes descriptive; if it changes sign, the crisis years were driving the raw correlation and the years responsible are named.

G is never confused with S, the country shock index in the folder beside this one. G is what everybody is exposed to at once, and it is removed. S is what one island was exposed to and its neighbours were not, a cyclone, an eruption, an epidemic, and it is kept and used, because it is the closest thing to an experiment these series contain. The two never share a term in any model.
